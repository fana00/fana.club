Beliefs

What we believe

Anyone can make.
The question is who grows.

I

The cost of making disappears.

Software, drama, advertising: one person finishes it in weeks. Making is open to everyone, so it is no longer an edge. One resource remains: how many times you can try.

II

The bottleneck moved to growth.

Made things are everywhere. Where they stall is bringing people in, keeping them, and getting them to pay. AI opened the door to making; the door to growth still stands before human judgment.

III

Scale no longer guarantees the win.

The production power scale once bought now belongs to individuals; what remains in the organization is slowness. A small company tries ten times while a large one gets one approval. So being tied to one thing is a loss.
