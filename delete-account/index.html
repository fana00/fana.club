Delete Account

This page explains how to delete your Aiya account, developed by Fanaai, Inc., and what happens to your data.

Delete in the app

  1. Open Aiya and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile > Settings > Delete account.
  3. Read the notice and tap Delete account to confirm.

Delete without the app

Email master@fana.club from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my account". We will confirm and complete the deletion within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, nickname and profile image
  • Watch history, likes, follows and settings
  • Your remaining coin balance and unlocked episodes

This is permanent. Remaining coins cannot be refunded or restored, except where the law requires.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Purchase and tax records, for the period required by law
  • Records needed to resolve disputes or prevent fraud, until they are resolved

All other information is deleted or anonymized within 30 days. Subscriptions bought through Apple or Google are not canceled automatically. Cancel them in your store settings.
